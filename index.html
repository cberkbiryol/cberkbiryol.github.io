About

I’m Berk Biryol, a passionate and results-driven Senior Software Engineer with a unique background bridging science and technology. Holding a Ph.D. in Geophysics & Seismology, I bring over 7 years of experience in full-stack web development, backend optimization, and cloud-native solutions.

My career has spanned projects in data-intensive environments, including large-scale database architecture, RESTful and SOAP APIs, performance tuning for SQL workloads, and containerized deployments using Docker, AWS, and CI/CD pipelines. I’ve had the privilege of providing technical consultation to organizations like the EPA and NIH, delivering high-quality solutions in regulated, mission-critical environments.

Beyond backend engineering, I’ve built modern front-end solutions using React, TypeScript, and GraphQL, created real-time dashboards for data visualization, and led initiatives to improve application performance, reducing load times by up to 10x in production systems. I enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams, mentoring junior developers, and driving technical solutions from concept to deployment.

Currently, I’m focused on building scalable, maintainable web applications while expanding my expertise in DevOps practices, testing automation, and cloud-native architecture. I’m passionate about tackling complex challenges, learning emerging technologies, and delivering software that makes an impact.
